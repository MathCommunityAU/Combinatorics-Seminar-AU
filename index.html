01 / About

The main aim of this seminar is to create a space where we can share topics, problems, and ideas in a relatively informal setting.

Presentations need not be on complete or finished work. They may explore work in progress, interesting problems, or open questions that we would like to investigate and possibly work on together.

The seminar meets once every two weeks, with each session lasting one hour. The time and venue will generally remain fixed throughout the semester.
